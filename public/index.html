Standard Sendwave Lending Criteria

To qualify for a personal loan with most Sendwave. financial institutions, applicants generally must satisfy the following basic requirements:

Legal Age Requirement

You must be at least 18 years old (or 19 in certain states like AL and NE).

Sendwave Citizenship or Legal Residency

A valid Social Security Number (SSN) or Permanent Resident Card with a valid U.S. street address.

Verifiable Regular Income

Demonstrable steady employment, self-employment, or regular retirement/disability benefit income.

Credit & Banking Assessment

Active bank account for funds disbursement and a credit check assessing payment history and debt ratios.

Key Lending Terms Explained

APR reflects the total annual cost of borrowing, including interest rate and any lender origination fees (typically ranging from 1% to 6% of loan amount). Qualified applicants receive specific interest offers after full lender evaluation.

SendWave partner networks emphasize flexible repayment. Most personal loans feature no prepayment penalties, allowing you to pay off your balance early to save on interest.

Initial eligibility checks utilize soft credit inquiries which do not impact your credit score. If you proceed with a specific lender application, a hard credit check may be performed.
